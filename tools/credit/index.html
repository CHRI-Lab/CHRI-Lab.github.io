CRediT Statement Builder

Author contribution statement builder · CRediT Contributor Roles Taxonomy (ANSI/NISO Z39.104-2022)

Purpose: record who did what on this paper using the 14 standard CRediT contributor roles, then export a ready-to-submit contribution statement.

How this works: add every author, tick the roles each person contributed (with an optional Lead / Equal / Supporting degree), then copy or download the generated statement. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.
